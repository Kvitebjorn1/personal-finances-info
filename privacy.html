Privacy Notice

Personal Finances is a private, non-commercial application used only by its owner to access and analyse the owner's own bank account data.

The application is not offered to third parties and does not provide a public financial service.

Bank account information obtained through the application is stored privately on infrastructure controlled by the owner for personal financial analysis.

The application does not sell, rent, publish, or share bank account information with third parties, except where technically necessary to obtain the data from the selected open-banking provider and bank.

Access can be revoked through the relevant bank or open-banking authorization process.
